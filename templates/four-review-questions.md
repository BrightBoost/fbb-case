# The four review questions

Use these on any AI-assisted deliverable, in this order.

| # | Question | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Direction:** does it answer the problem we set, for this audience? | Compare with the brief. Does each part serve the question? |
| 2 | **Roots:** does every claim trace to a source that exists and says this? | Open the source. Find the passage. Check the version and date. |
| 3 | **Numbers:** can each figure be recomputed, and do totals reconcile? | Recompute ratios from their parts. Add up every total. Compare figures across slides. |
| 4 | **Focus:** what does the audience need, and what can go? | Count the slides that don't serve the decision. |

## Failure patterns to watch for

- Fabricated references: plausible title or number, no such source
- Outdated regulatory references: an old or draft version presented as current
- Confident but wrong calculations: ratios and totals that don't match their parts
- Fabricated statistics: a convincing number without a source you can open
- Real source, wrong claim: the source exists but doesn't say this
- Lost focus: the work answers a broader question than the one asked
