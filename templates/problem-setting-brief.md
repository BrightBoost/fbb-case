# Problem-setting brief

Fill this in **before** anyone opens an AI tool. A good brief takes 10–15 minutes and saves hours of review.

## 1. The question
What exactly does the client need answered? Write it as one question.

>

## 2. The audience
Who reads this, what do they already know, and what decision do they have to take?

>

## 3. The format
How many slides or pages at most? What goes in the main part, and what goes in the appendix?

>

## 4. The numbers that matter
Which three to five figures does the audience need to see? Where does each one come from?

## 5. Out of scope
What should the work deliberately **not** cover?

>

## 6. Evidence rules
What evidence does each claim need? For example: "Every regulatory claim cites the document and section, with a quote." "Every number can be recomputed from the client data."

>

## 7. Checks before it comes to me
What should the junior check, or have an agent check, before handing the work over?

- [ ] Every reference opened and quoted
- [ ] Every number recomputed, totals reconciled
- [ ] Recommendations checked against the client's own policies
- [ ] Each slide answers the question in section 1
