Two things to watch:

- The ECB published a **draft** of its guide for consultation in July 2023 and the **final** version in May 2024. Use the final version.
- EU legal texts have amendments. Check that you are reading the consolidated version that applies today.

## BCBS 239 at a glance

BCBS 239 has 14 principles in four groups. Principles 1 to 11 apply to banks; 12 to 14 are for supervisors.

| Group | Principles |
| --- | --- |
| Overarching governance and infrastructure | 1 Governance · 2 Data architecture and IT infrastructure |
| Risk data aggregation capabilities | 3 Accuracy and integrity · 4 Completeness · 5 Timeliness · 6 Adaptability |
| Risk reporting practices | 7 Accuracy · 8 Comprehensiveness · 9 Clarity and usefulness · 10 Frequency · 11 Distribution |
| Supervisory review, tools and cooperation | 12 Review · 13 Remedial actions and supervisory measures · 14 Home/host cooperation |

## The ECB guide at a glance

The ECB guide sets out supervisory expectations in seven areas (chapter 3):

| Section | Topic |
| --- | --- |
| 3.1 | Responsibilities of the management body |
| 3.2 | Sufficient scope of application |
| 3.3 | Effective data governance framework |
| 3.4 | Integrated data architecture |
| 3.5 | Group-wide data quality management and standards |
| 3.6 | Timeliness of internal risk reporting |
| 3.7 | Effective implementation programmes |

The ECB guide uses numbered points within each section rather than paragraph numbers. Cite it as, for example, "ECB guide, section 3.5, point 4".
