## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy sets out how Fake Bright Bank N.V. ("FBB") governs the data used in its internal risk reports, so that risk data is accurate, complete and available on time for decision-making.

1.2 The policy supports FBB's compliance with the Basel Committee's Principles for effective risk data aggregation and risk reporting (BCBS 239).

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy applies to credit risk data of Fake Bright Bank N.V.

2.2 Liquidity risk data is governed by the Treasury department in line with its own procedures.

2.3 The subsidiaries FBB Lease B.V. and Bright Bank Deutschland GmbH are out of scope. They deliver their figures to the group in the format agreed with Group Risk.

## 3. Governance and approval

3.1 The Chief Risk Officer approves this policy and the data governance framework that implements it.

3.2 The Chief Risk Officer informs the Management Board of material changes to this policy once a year.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

4.1 **Data owners.** Each critical data element has a data owner. Data owners are senior managers in a business line or in the risk function. They are accountable end to end for the quality of their data: definitions, controls, accuracy, completeness, timeliness and remediation of issues. Data owners may delegate tasks, but not accountability.

4.2 **Data custodian.** Group IT acts as data custodian. It runs the systems that store and move risk data. Group IT is never the data owner.

4.3 **Data Quality Committee.** The Data Quality Committee is chaired by the Chief Risk Officer. It meets quarterly, reviews data quality indicators and decides on escalated issues.

4.4 **Data users.** Users of risk data report any data quality issue they find to the data owner.

## 5. Data quality standards

5.1 Each business line sets its own data quality tolerance thresholds for the data it owns, based on its own risk appetite.

5.2 Data quality is measured on four dimensions: accuracy, completeness, timeliness and consistency.

## 6. Data quality management

6.1 Data owners run data quality controls on their critical data elements at least monthly.

6.2 Group-wide data quality standards and tolerance thresholds are set by the Chief Data Officer and apply to all business lines.

6.3 Breaches of tolerance thresholds are escalated to the Data Quality Committee.

6.4 The Data Quality Committee is chaired by the Chief Data Officer.

6.5 All data quality issues are recorded in the data issues register, with severity, root cause, owner, remediation deadline and evidence of closure.

## 7. Reconciliation

7.1 Risk data is reconciled monthly with the source systems from which it is extracted.

7.2 Differences above EUR 5 million are explained by the data owner.

## 8. Manual processes and end-user computing

8.1 Spreadsheets and other end-user computing tools used in risk reporting are the responsibility of the individual user who built them.

8.2 Users keep a copy of each monthly version of their spreadsheets.

## 9. Risk reporting

9.1 The group risk report is delivered to the Management Board quarterly. A monthly risk dashboard is delivered to the CRO.

9.2 The monthly risk dashboard is delivered within 30 working days after month-end.

9.3 In times of stress the reporting frequency remains unchanged, to ensure consistency and comparability of the figures.

9.4 Ad hoc data requests are handled by Group Risk within ten working days.

## 10. Data architecture

10.1 Each department maintains its own data definitions for the data it uses in risk reporting.

10.2 Group IT documents the main data flows into the risk data warehouse.

## 11. Monitoring, validation and audit

11.1 Compliance with this policy is self-assessed annually by the data owners. The results are reported to the Data Quality Committee.

## 12. Key risk indicators

12.1 The key risk indicators in the group risk report, and their thresholds, are listed in Annex B.

12.2 Treasury sets the internal early-warning threshold for the liquidity coverage ratio at 120%.
